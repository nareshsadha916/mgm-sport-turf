System Architecture Highlights

Zero Double-Booking Engine & Instant Settlement

Engineered for high-traffic weekends, rain-friendly cancellations, and automated split payouts.

1

Instant Redis Lock

Selecting a court instantly acquires a 10-minute atomic hold key in Redis. No other user can proceed to payment for that exact slot.

2

PostgreSQL ACID Protection

Database enforces partial unique indexes (court_id, date, slot_start) WHERE status IN ('PENDING','CONFIRMED').

3

Razorpay Route Split Payouts

Automated marketplace split routes 95% revenue to the turf owner's bank account instantly while retaining platform commission.

4

Fair Cancellation & Auto-Refunds

Full 100% refund for >24h cancellations, 50% for 6-24h, and 100% automatic refund on bad weather / turf maintenance.
